NBA Approach

The TV Networks/streaming platforms would have two main media networks plus an online streaming service. The proposed networks and streaming services are the following:

Primary Network Streaming Partners
ESPN/ABC ESPN DTC
NBC Peacock
YouTube YouTube
DTC = Direct-to-Consumer

For TV Networks and streaming, the process is simplified regardless of the quarterfinal and semifinal rotation being a 3-year, 4-year, or 5-year cycle.

First Two Rounds

ESPN/ABC and NBC would each have six first round games. NBC will be able to distribute two of those six first round games to NBCSN. ESPN would exclusively air two first round games and ABC would have four first round games. ESPN/ABC and NBC would each have three second round games each as well. YouTube, on the other hand, would have a total of six exclusive playoff games for the first two rounds that would be streamed on the College Football Playoff YouTube Channel. Here is how the games would be distributed with the first two rounds:

First Round

Regardless of utilizing a 3-year, 4-year, or 5-year cycle for the quarterfinal and semifinal rotation, the distribution would look something like this:

Primary Friday First Round Games (in EST) Saturday First Round Games (in EST)
ESPN/ABC/ESPN DTC One Friday Evening/Night Game Five on Saturday (Two on ESPN, Three on ABC)
NBC/Peacock One Friday Evening/Night Game Five on Saturday (Two exclusively on NBCSN/Peacock, Three on NBC/Peacock)
YouTube Two Friday Evening/Night Games Two on Saturday

In addition to YouTube having four exclusive playoff games in the first round, they would also have the right to simulcast playoff games from ESPN/ABC and from NBC/Pecock on the College Football Playoff YouTube Channel. The rest of the playoff rounds would be different depending on whether using a 3-year, 4-year, or 5-year cycle for the quarterfinal and semifinal rotation.

Second Round

Regardless of utilizing a 3-year, 4-year, or 5-year cycle for the quarterfinal and semifinal rotation, YouTube would have two second round games on Wednesday. ESPN/ABC/ESPN DTC and NBC/Peacock would rotate to air either two Friday second round games or two Saturday second round games. Neither would be able to air Wednesday Night games because ESPN airs the NBA doubleheaders on Wednesdays during the NBA season (from October - April). Here is the breakdown of how many second-round games each of the proposed networks would air/stream:

Primary Version A Version B
ESPN/ABC/ESPN DTC 1 on Friday, 2 on Saturday 2 on Friday, 1 on Saturday
NBC/Peacock 2 on Friday, 1 on Saturday 1 on Friday, 2 on Saturday
YouTube 2 on Wednesday 2 on Wednesday
