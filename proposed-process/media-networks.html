Proposed Media Networks

Why Multi-Network Distribution Matters

Distributing playoff games across multiple networks maximizes revenue for schools and conferences while ensuring broad fan access regardless of cable provider or streaming service. A 32-team playoff generates 31 games across five rounds, which is far more inventory than any single network could handle. Partnering with the correct media networks is critical in order for the playoff to reach the widest possible audience and to create competitive bidding that drives up rights fees.

Overview

There are a few ways to distribute 31 playoff games. I would strongly recommend doing something similar to the NFL's approach because this has been proven successful in maximizing both revenue and viewership. However, there can be other ways to make sure the 32-team playoff field can reach the widest possible audience and create competitive bidding to drive up rights fees.
