Secondary Playoff System Version A

In this version, we would see that the top 12 non-CFP bowl games serve as consolation games for elminated playoff participants. This means that the top 12 non-CFP bowl games are already taken and the College Playoff Crown does not have many options of bowl games to use as quarterfinal and semifinal sites. Although the cupboard becomes mostly bare, there are still some bowl games that can still be useful as College Playoff Crown Sites. In addition, the College Playoff Crown would also have the option to either utilize consolation bowl games or forgo them.

Upsides and Downsides

There are upsides and downsides for this version of the payout.

Upsides

If Consolation Bowl Games happen in both playoff fields, it can provide advantages. Doing this version would work for the following reasons:

  • The payout distribution is the highest
  • You can still provide the right number of bowl-eligible slots without compromising
  • Additional Postseason Inventory

These can provide upsides to how the bowl games can be distributed. With a very high payout, there would be no shortage of teams to collect payouts for their conferences for every round that they advance or land in a consolation bowl game.

Downsides

Although this version would be a very cool addition, we also have the consider the downsides. Basically, the disadvantages are the following:

  • All the top bowl games (including consolation games for eliminated first and second round FBS playoff participants) are within the 32-team FBS playoff field
  • Pool of remaining stand-alone bowl games are not impressive
  • Rotational CPC Bowl Games are less stellar because top bowl games were already taken

Alhough the payout distribution is the most, the bowl quality of both the CPC and stand-alone games are poor. In addition, because many of the bowl games would be utilized as consolation games, there would not be many stand-alone bowl games remaining to select the best remaining bowl-eligible teams. In addition, if more stand-alone bowl games were added, the quality would remain poor as those bowl games are unproven in prestige and could have less durations.
