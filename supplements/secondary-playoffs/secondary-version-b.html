Secondary Playoff System Version B

In this version, we would see that the top four non-CFP bowl games serve as consolation games for elminated second round playoff participants. This means the top four non-CFP bowl games are already taken and the College Playoff Crown has a bit more options of bowl games to use as quarterfinal and semifinal sites. Although the bowl games become better, there are still some bowl games that can still be useful as College Playoff Crown Sites. In addition, the College Playoff Crown would also have the option to either utilize consolation bowl games or forgo them.

Upsides and Downsides

There are upsides and downsides for this version of the payout.

Upsides

If Consolation Bowl Games happen in both playoff fields, doing this version would work for the following reasons:

  • Highest payout distribution using 32-team playoff field + 4 consolation bowl games
  • Still provides the right number of bowl-eligible slots without compromising
  • First round games remain as high-stakes

Version B of the secondary playoff field provides a middle-ground balance of making sure the bowl game quality is still good in the College Playoff Crown as well as the stand-alone bowl games. The high payout distribution from the 32-team FBS playoff field plus 4 consolation games is a very nice bonus. In addition, the high stakes would remain in the first round of both the 32-team FBS playoff and 16-team CPC. Teams have to win their first round game to at least have a good quality bowl game, or their season is over if eliminated.

Downsides

Although this version would be a very cool addition, we also have the consider the downsides. Basically, the disadvantages are the following:

  • Pool of Bowl-Elgible Teams starts bordering on being saturated
  • Available Stand-Alone Bowl Games starts becoming scarce if utilizing consolation bowl games for both CPC and FBS playoffs

Alhough the payout distribution is higher than Version A, the downsides stem from being on the fence of either saturating the bowl season or not having enough bowls for the remaining bowl games. If CPC uses consolation games, there would only be 16 bowl games remaining (or less if bowl games discontinue) since a total of 20 bowl games would already be taken. If CPC forgoes consolation games, 20 bowl games would still remain; however, that would begin inviting teams with losing records to participate.
