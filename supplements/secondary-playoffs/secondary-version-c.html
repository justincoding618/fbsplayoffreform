Secondary Playoff System Version C

In this version, we would see that there would be no consolation games for playoff participants. This means the top non-CFP bowl games can be utilized in the College Playoff Crown. Having the top non-CFP bowl games means the College Playoff Crown becomes the most prestigous with their quarterfinal and semifinal sites for this version. It also means that teams will have a reason to play in the secondary playoff in case they miss the playoffs. Once again, the College Playoff Crown would also have the option to either utilize consolation bowl games or forgo them.

Upsides and Downsides

There are upsides and downsides for this version of the payout.

Upsides

Without utilzing bowl games as consolation games, it can provide advantages. Doing this version would work for the following reasons:

  • Each round of the 32-team FBS playoff and 16-team CPC has more stakes
  • Increased payouts of both the 32-team FBS playoff field and 16-team CPC
  • The bowl games in the 16-team CPC is the most prestigous out of the different possible versions
  • A lot of Postseason Inventory for Stand-Alone Bowl Games

This version provides the best of both worlds. It provides stakes, best bowl quality within the CPC, and more inventory for teams that neither made the FBS playoffs or secondary would help the remaining bowl-eligible teams. This is a good solution for anyone that wants to see best quality of each playoff field and in the stand-alone games.

Downsides

Although this version would be a very cool addition, we also have the consider the downsides. Basically, the only disadvantage is the following:

  • Some bowl games may likely have to be cut to keep the number of bowl-eligible slots reasonable

Without making bowl games as consolation games, some bowl games would have to be cut in order to not make bowl season saturated. For example, let's say we still want to keep 80 bowl-eligible slots. With 48 teams participating in either the 32-team FBS playoff or 16-team CPC, only the 16 remaining bowl games out of 20 (if CPC uses consolation games) or 24 (if CPC forgoes consolation games) can be kept. In addition, if more stand-alone bowl games were added, it becomes counterintuitive because it would allow teams with losing records to participate.
