32-Team Playoff + Secondary Playoff System Only

Let's say we want to implement the 32-team playoff plus the consolation bowl games for second round participants (while providing the option for Wildcard Bowl Consolation games for first round playoff partipants) and the secondary playoff system. The bowl lineups for those two systems would stay the same. However, without the implementation of the tertiary playoff system, we would be left with the following bowl games:

Bowl Games
First Responder Bowl Frisco Bowl Boca Raton Bowl
Puerto Rico Bowl Cure Bowl Salute to Veterans Bowl
Arizona Bowl Myrtle Beach Bowl XBox Bowl

Overall Breakdown

By implementing 32 out of the 41 currently existing bowl games, that would leave us with about 9 stand-alone bowl games that would take teams that neither made it to the FBS playoffs nor CPC. In total, it would look like the following:

Even though the tertiary playoff would not be implemented, we see that the same number of teams would be participating in the postseason. While this would be fine in certain cases, if the number of bowl games continue to grow, implementing a tertiary playoff field may become necessary.

Basically, we have 31 FBS playoff games (including the six bowl games hosting two of the last three rounds), plus 4 consolation bowl games for second round FBS playoff participants or 12 consolation bowl games for first round and second round FBS playoff participants, 31 College Playoff Crown Games (CPC) Games (including the 14 bowl games that would host the final three rounds), and eight consolation bowl games for the CPC participants. Then, we would have eight stand-alone bowl games to work with. In total, that gives us either 83 or 91 postseason games depending on whether to have consolation bowl games for first round FBS playoff participants.
